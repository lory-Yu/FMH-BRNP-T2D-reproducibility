## Code availability

The analysis code, reproducible workflows, environment specifications, and
instructions required to reproduce the publicly redistributable components of
this study are available at [GitHub repository URL] and will be archived at
Zenodo upon release [Zenodo DOI].

## Data availability

Study-generated derived outputs, RCT meta-analysis inputs, accession manifests,
source-data tables for reproducible figures, input schemas, checksums, and
reconstruction instructions are provided in the accompanying repository.
Certain row-level integrated inputs derived from third-party databases are not
redistributed because their source terms do not clearly establish
redistribution permission. The relevant source databases, provenance, schemas,
and reconstruction procedures are documented to enable researchers with
appropriate access to reconstruct these inputs locally.

The RCT meta-analysis and Figures 2–6 can be regenerated directly from the
released materials. Complete from-scratch execution of the metabolism-aware v2
workflow additionally requires reconstruction of the non-redistributed
third-party database-derived transformation input.
