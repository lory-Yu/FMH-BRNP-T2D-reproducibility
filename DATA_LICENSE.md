# Data-licence scope

Status: **FINAL CO-AUTHOR APPROVAL CONFIRMED — 13 SEPTEMBER 2026**

The licence for data products explicitly identified as both author-generated
and redistributable in this release is the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0). The full legal text is at
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode>.

## Included scope

CC BY 4.0 covers only study-generated aggregate analysis outputs,
author-generated figure source data, and reproducibility records for which the
authors hold the necessary rights. Inclusion in the repository alone does not
override third-party rights or source-provider terms.

## Explicit exclusions

CC BY 4.0 does not cover:

- third-party data or third-party database content;
- commercial or restricted database exports;
- database-derived row-level records whose redistribution rights are unclear;
- `herb_transformation_paths_v2.tsv` or complete/minimal versions of
  `2_3_herb_compound_clean.csv`;
- GBD/IHME or IDF source extracts;
- RCT full texts;
- FASTQ/FQ reads, SILVA files or AGORA2 model files;
- external software, models or databases governed by their original terms;
- signed assessments, reviewer-specific records or other internal documents.

Third-party materials remain subject to their providers' licences and terms.
The source databases, provenance, required schemas and reconstruction
procedures are documented without granting redistribution rights.
